"Settler colonialism" isn't a historical category. It's a political weapon, and in practice it comes down to one rule: colonialism is when "white"/European-ancestry people do it. Everyone else's conquests get filed under history. Europe's get filed under crimes.

## A category with one member

Apply the term consistently and it falls apart. Turkey sits on land that was Greek, Armenian, and Byzantine until Turkic peoples rode in from Central Asia and took it. Thailand's Tai peoples migrated south and displaced or absorbed whoever was already there. The Arab conquests turned North Africa and the Levant Arab. The Han spread across southern China over centuries, and Japan pushed the Ainu to the margins of their own islands. Nobody calls Turkey or Thailand settler-colonial states, and nobody is demanding they be decolonised.

The reason is simple. The point was never to describe the past. The point is to attack the West.

## Every land was taken

Every piece of land on the planet has been conquered at one time or another, almost always by force. Peoples moved, fought, won, lost, mixed, and moved again. That isn't a Western aberration. It's the normal shape of human history. Likewise, almost every culture alive today has been enslaved at some point, and almost every one has owned slaves.

If historians were honest, they would admit that nearly every country is settler-colonial. The ones that aren't are the exceptions.

## Even England

Take England itself. The name means "land of the Angles", after the Anglo-Saxons who crossed from what is now Germany and overpowered the Britons. A few centuries later the Normans crossed from France, on land they had taken after migrating from Scandinavia, and conquered it all over again. By the standards applied to America, England is a settler colony twice over.

Nobody teaches it that way, because the framework isn't built to explain England. It's built to indict it.

## The refusal to judge

The same academics who condemn Europe without limit refuse to judge anyone else. Cultural and moral relativism means Western civilisation is held to an absolute standard and every other one is held to none.

But some judgements are just true. The Aztecs were barbarians. They enslaved their neighbours with particular cruelty and practised human sacrifice and ritual cannibalism on an industrial scale. The Soviet Union and its satellites were corrupt totalitarian regimes that murdered millions. A historian who can name Europe's sins but goes quiet about these isn't teaching history. They're pushing an agenda, and the left-wing slant of the universities makes that agenda far more powerful than most people realise.

## What decolonisation is for

Seen this way, "decolonisation" stops being about the past and becomes a project for the present. It works by demoralising the people it targets. Teach a people that their existence on their own land is a crime, and they lose the confidence to defend it. Guilt becomes the argument for mass migration, and mass migration becomes a kind of colonisation in reverse. The pendulum of politics currently sees an advantage in propaganda against white European colonisers, and it is using it.

Westerners who sign up to this aren't being moral. They're masochists, obsessed with casting themselves as the villains of history, and they want to drag everyone else down with them.

Every people on earth stands on conquered ground. Only one is being told to apologise for it.
