Ludwig II of Bavaria financed some of the most beautiful castles and palaces in Europe for about $347 million, or 0.04% of Elon's net worth.

Elon lives like a man with 1% of his net worth.

The aristocrat and the bourgeois are different species of men. Let no one conflate the two, or blur them together under the term "ruling class".
